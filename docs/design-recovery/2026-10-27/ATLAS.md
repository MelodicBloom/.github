### M03 — Tokens and parameters: typed, scoped, and bound to behavior

**Name***

M03 — Tokens and parameters: typed, scoped, and bound to behavior

**Use when***

**Content***

[Proposed model] Separate primitive values, semantic roles, family material recipes, component state bindings, contextual profiles, and user overrides. ## Patterns rejected or deferred

- One “premium” glass style for every project: erases material/narrative distinctions.
- One animation library or spring preset for every behavior: ignores task and material character.
- “Everything is physics”: confuses metaphor, visual approximation, simulation, and measurement.
- All specs must be implemented before anything is useful: blocks usable partial instruments.
- A successful slice is the finished product: loses the horizon and remaining user value.
- A generated URL, booking record, or unlock is a fulfilled external outcome: lacks the necessary boundary evidence.
- Every ambition becomes a mandatory cross-repository schema: creates premature infrastructure.
- A beautiful graph or inspector is sufficient product experience: leaves desire, usefulness, and agency unserved.
